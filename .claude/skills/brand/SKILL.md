# Brand

## Fonts

From Google Fonts:

- **Headings and body**: Instrument Sans
- **Accent (a tagline or a quote)**: Instrument Serif
- **Code, numbers and identifiers**: JetBrains Mono

## Colors

The color scheme for this product:

- **Primary**: Sea Green `#06D6A0`, Midnight text
- **Secondary**: Marigold `#FFC145`, Midnight text
- **Tertiary**: Coral `#F78C6B`, Midnight text
- **Information**: Cerulean `#118AB2`, white text on its darker shade
- **Highlight or alert**: Raspberry `#EF476F`, white text on its darker shade
- **Ink on light backgrounds**: Midnight `#073B4C`
- **Ink on dark backgrounds**: Sea Mist `#CDD8DB`

Use Sea Green, Marigold and Coral when three things sit side by side, such as pipeline stages or chart series. Add Cerulean fourth and Raspberry fifth.

### Darker shades for light backgrounds

The colors above are too light for small text and thin lines on white. White text on Cerulean or Raspberry is below WCAG AA too. Use these darker shades of the same colors for text, lines, icons, and badges with white text. Each one reaches 4.5:1 on white and on the light page background.

| Color | Darker shade |
|---|---|
| Sea Green | `#008050` |
| Marigold | `#9B6200` |
| Coral | `#B65132` |
| Cerulean | `#00789F` |
| Raspberry | `#D22758` |

Keep the brand colors for fills with Midnight text, large shapes and chart areas. When a chart uses a light fill, show its value in text next to it.

### Lighter shades for dark backgrounds

On the dark page and black panels, use these shades for text, lines and icons. Each one reaches 4.5:1 on both. Badges with white text keep the darker shades above, because white text needs a dark fill.

| Color | Lighter shade |
|---|---|
| Sea Green | `#06D6A0` |
| Marigold | `#FFC145` |
| Coral | `#F78C6B` |
| Cerulean | `#4BB8DF` |
| Raspberry | `#F4769A` |

Sea Green, Marigold and Coral fills keep Midnight text in dark mode.

## Surfaces

- **Light mode**: page background `#F0F4F5`, white panels and cards
- **Dark mode**: page background `#04232E`, black panels and cards

Panels are rounded with no outline. Sea Green, Marigold and Coral are too bright for small text, so use their darker shades on light backgrounds and lighten them on dark ones.

## Avoid

Avoid borders on one edge of panels, or single-line borders at top of page.
Avoid over-use of gradient fills.

## Tone

Plain business English. Short labels, no jargon, no exclamation marks. Money is always shown with its currency.
Down-to-earth language without LLM smells like "It's not X. It's Y." and overly profound pithy statements where not needed.
